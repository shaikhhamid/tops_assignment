Welcome to My Zomato Page

Features

Zomato’s Food Rescue feature allows nearby app users to claim and buy fresh, untouched canceled food orders at a discounted price within a 3 km radius of the delivery partner

Why I Use It

  • Wide Restaurant Discovery: Browse thousands of local menus, photos, and verified customer ratings before deciding what to eat or where to dine out.
  • Doorstep Delivery: Order food from a massive network of eateries, including places that do not have their own dedicated delivery staff.
  • Smart Recommendations: Use AI-driven suggestions tailored to your past order history, favorite cuisines, and trending dishes nearby.
  • Discounts and Offers: Save money using frequent in-app promotions, bank discounts, or membership perks like Zomato Gold.
  • Order Tracking and Support: Follow your delivery rider's live location on a map and get quick customer service help for missing or wrong items.
  • Specialized Features: Utilize handy additions like ordering food directly to your seat while traveling on select trains via IRCTC partnerships.
